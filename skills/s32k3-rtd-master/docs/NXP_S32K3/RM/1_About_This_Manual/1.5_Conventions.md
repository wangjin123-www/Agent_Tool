# 1.5 Conventions 

## 1.5.1 Notes and cautions 

Specific information is provided as part of notes and cautions throughout this manual. 

~~<mark>NOTE</mark>~~ 

Emphasizes information that deserves extra attention. 

~~<mark>CAUTION</mark>~~ 

Informs you of situations that could lead to highly undesirable outcomes—such as damage to the chip or irreversible malfunction. 

## 1.5.2 Numbering systems 

The following suffixes identify different numbering systems: 

Table 1. Numbering systems 

|This suffix|Identifies a|
|---|---|
|b|Binary number. For example, the binary equivalent of the number 5 is mentioned as 101b. In some<br>cases, 0b is prefixed to binary numbers.|
|d|Decimal number. Decimal numbers are followed by this suffix only when there is a possibility of<br>confusion. In general, decimal numbers are used without a suffix.|
|h|Hexadecimal number. For example, the hexadecimal equivalent of the number 60 is mentioned as<br>3Ch. In some cases, 0x is prefixed to hexadecimal numbers.|
